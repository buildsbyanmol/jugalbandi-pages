A small note on privacy.

How this website and beta registration handle your information.

Beta registration

The beta signup uses Google Forms to collect your email address and computer preference: macOS, Windows, or Linux. We use these details to contact you about Jugalbandi beta access and launch updates.

The form loads when you open the beta panel. You can also open it directly on Google Forms. Your response goes to Google Forms when you submit it. Google manages the form validation, response storage, and submission confirmation. This website does not independently confirm or copy your response into a separate signup database.

Google's Privacy Policy applies to its handling of form and browser information.

Illustrative interactions

The workspace, quota panels, terminal snippets, event records, decisions, and close ritual use synthetic sample data. They do not connect to your repositories, harnesses, terminals, vendor accounts, or local Jugalbandi service. Demo decisions change only the displayed mockup.

Website requests

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Opening the beta panel also loads the Google Forms embed. These requests connect your browser to Google's services. A GitHub Pages deployment is also subject to GitHub's hosting and request logging policies.

Local product architecture

The planned product's local logs and cross-vendor controls are separate from this website. A claim that the local record stays on your laptop does not mean connected model providers process all requests offline.
